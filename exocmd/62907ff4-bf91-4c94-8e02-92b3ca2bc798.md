## ⛔ Why the Task parent rule lives HERE and not on the "Create action" grounding

`mergeInheritanceRules` (`packages/core/src/services/UniversalDefaultTemplateResolver.ts`) deduplicates **by `targetPropertyName`**: it builds a `Map` over the grounding's rule list (last entry wins), overrides every same-property universal rule with it, and then skips the remaining grounding rules carrying that property. Consequently a grounding can express **at most one** rule per property, and that one shadows all universal rules for the same property.

This template's own list is **not** deduplicated against itself, so sibling condition-based rules for the same property (entries 6 and 7) coexist here and each fires on its own target class. That is why grounding `1bc1e938` ("Create action") declares **no** parent rule at all — measured on a vault copy: declaring both there loses whichever rule is not last.

⚠ Consequence for future edits: adding a `ems__Effort_parent` rule to any grounding silently disables **both** entries 6 and 7 for that command. The five other effort-creating groundings (`a222094b`, `8748f8b0`, `4919afb1`, `b7a84c3c`, `d53c8726`) do declare `43731bae` and are therefore unaffected by entry 7 — verified by running `create-task` / `create-project` before and after.
