# exocmd__Grounding_targetQuery

`exo__ObjectProperty` on `exocmd__Grounding` (cardinality 0..1).

TARGET-source for `property_set` groundings (req `faf269bf`). YAML serialization: `targetQuery: "[[<query__NamedQuery-uid>]]"`.

Answers **which asset the step writes into** — as distinct from `exocmd__Grounding_targetValueQuery`, which answers **which value it writes**. The two are orthogonal and compose freely.

The referenced `query__NamedQuery` runs read-only with `$currentAsset` auto-injected = the click-target, and its first projection identifies the target asset. The engine takes that binding's raw IRI and converts it to the asset's vault path.

## Why it exists

A step could address exactly two things: the click-target, and the asset a previous step created. A **reified statement** is neither — it must be found from one of its ends. Recording state about a relationship (rather than on one of its participants) was therefore impossible without denormalising that state onto a participant.

## Mutual exclusivity

Disjoint in effect with `exocmd__Grounding_targetsCreatedInstance` — both address the step's TARGET, so setting both is refused by the executor rather than silently arbitrated.

⚠ No `owl__propertyDisjointWith` axiom is recorded for that pair because `exocmd__Grounding_targetsCreatedInstance` is **not declared** as an `exo__Property` (nor is `exocmd__Grounding_cloneTargetBody`); a wikilink to a non-existent asset would dangle. The constraint lives in `GroundingExecutor.executePropertySet` and is covered by the requirement's Scenario 2. Declaring those two fields would let the axiom be recorded here.

## Fail-loud

A query that matches nothing fails the step. The click-target is deliberately **not** a fallback: writing there would silently put the value on the wrong asset.

## Consumer

Agreement audit (RFC `85fa0652`) — the «last audited» state is kept as a reified link rather than a field on the norm itself; the button updates the link found from the norm.
